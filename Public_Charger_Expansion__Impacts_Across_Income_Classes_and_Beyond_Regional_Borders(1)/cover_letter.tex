Dear Editors,

We write to express our great interest in contributing a full-length article entitled \textbf{``Public Charger Expansion: Impacts Across Income Classes and Beyond Regional Borders''} to the journal \textit{Transportation Research Part D: Transport and Environment}.

This manuscript addresses how public charging infrastructure networks can be designed to support the adoption of zero-emission passenger cars by consumer groups of \textit{all} income levels, and, moreover, the impact of the local charging infrastructure expansion on the BEV adoption of consumers living in neighboring regions. We demonstrate this using data for the Basque Country, a federal state in the North of Spain.

We are confident that this manuscript is well aligned with the scope of the journal by addressing transportation infrastructure needs to support the reduction of carbon emissions by the private passenger car sector. 

The most important novelties encompass both a methodological development of optimization-based charging infrastructure planning and the analysis of different degrees of charging network density on consumer groups of different income levels. Moreover, to the best of our knowledge, we are the first to explicitly address the spatial spill-over of charging infrastructure in a techno-economic framework.

The manuscript ``Public Charger Expansion: Impacts Across Income Classes and Beyond Regional Borders'' has not been submitted or published previously to any other scientific journal. All authors, Antonia Golab, Sebastian Zwickl-Bernhard, and Hans Auer, have approved the submission to the journal. The authors declare no conflicts of interest to disclose concerning the study.

Thank you very much for your valuable time.
